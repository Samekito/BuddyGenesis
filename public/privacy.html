SOC Buddy Privacy Policy

Effective 30 September 2026

SOC Buddy is an AI assistant that answers questions about the School of Computing at the Federal University of Technology Akure (FUTA), Nigeria, using the School's departmental handbooks. It is available at soc-buddy.onrender.com.

SOC Buddy is an independent project by Olusola Iyanuoluwa Samuel Junior, built for students of the School of Computing, FUTA. It is not an official FUTA service.

This policy explains what information SOC Buddy collects, how it is used, and the choices you have.

Information we collect

  • Google account details, if you sign in with Google: your name, email address and profile picture link, as provided by Google. Your email address identifies your account.
  • Sign-up details, if you create an account with an email and password: your name, email address and a securely hashed form of your password (the password itself is never stored). This account is separate from any Google sign-in that uses the same email address.
  • Account emails: to confirm a new account or reset a password, SOC Buddy emails a one-time link to the address given. Only a scrambled (hashed) form of each link is stored, and links expire after 24 hours (confirmation) or one hour (password reset). SOC Buddy sends no newsletters or marketing.
  • Your conversations: the questions you ask, SOC Buddy's answers, conversation titles, timestamps, and any thumbs-up/down feedback you give. These are saved so you can return to past chats.
  • A login cookie that keeps you signed in for up to one day. SOC Buddy uses no advertising or analytics cookies.
  • Technical logs: our hosting provider may record standard request information such as IP addresses and times. SOC Buddy's own logs do not record the content of your messages.
  • Abuse protection: to limit repeated sign-in and sign-up attempts, SOC Buddy briefly keeps your IP address and the email you sign in with in memory only (never on disk). They are discarded soon after the limit periods (up to one hour) end, and whenever the app restarts. It also counts how many questions each account asks per day.

How we use it

  • To sign you in and show you your own chat history.
  • To answer your questions. Each question, together with your recent messages in that conversation and relevant handbook passages, is sent to an AI model to generate the answer.
  • To keep the service working and fix problems.

We do not sell your information, show you ads, or use your data to track you across other websites or apps.

Services that process your data

SOC Buddy relies on these providers, which process data on its behalf. Their own privacy policies also apply.

  • Groq (groq.com): runs the AI model. Receives your questions, recent conversation messages and handbook passages to produce answers.
  • Neon (neon.com): database that stores your account details and chat history, on servers in the United States.
  • Brevo (brevo.com): sends account emails (sign-up confirmation and password reset). Receives your email address, your name and the email's content.
  • Render (render.com): hosts the application, on servers in the United States.
  • Google: provides "Sign in with Google". SOC Buddy only receives the account details listed above.

Google user data

SOC Buddy requests only your basic Google profile and email address, and uses them solely to sign you in and keep your chat history under your account. SOC Buddy's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. This information is not shared with anyone except the service providers listed above, and is never used for advertising.

How long we keep it

Your account and conversations are kept until you delete them or ask us to, or until SOC Buddy is shut down. You can delete individual conversations yourself from the chat history sidebar.

Your choices and rights

  • Delete your data: email us to delete your account and all your conversations.
  • See your data: email us for a copy of the information stored about you.
  • Revoke Google access at any time from your Google Account permissions page.

We will respond to requests within 30 days.

Security

Connections to SOC Buddy and to its database are encrypted (HTTPS/TLS), and access to stored data is limited to the project's developer. No online service can be perfectly secure, so please avoid sharing sensitive personal information (such as passwords or financial details) in your questions.

Children

SOC Buddy is intended for university students and is not directed at children under 13.

Accuracy of answers

Answers are generated by AI from the handbooks and can be wrong or out of date. Always confirm important details with your department.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
