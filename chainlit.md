# SOC Buddy - Your Intelligent School of Computing Assistant 🎓💻

Welcome to **SOC Buddy**, your friendly and intelligent assistant for the School of Computing (SOC) at the Federal University of Technology, Akure (FUTA), Nigeria. SOC Buddy is designed to assist students, staff, and visitors by providing accurate, relevant, and timely information, answering queries, and offering support for various SOC-related tasks and concepts.

## Features 🌟

- **Real-time Q&A**: Ask SOC Buddy any question related to the School of Computing, and get instant, accurate answers.
- **Course Information 📚**: Detailed information about courses offered, schedules, syllabi, and prerequisites.
- **Administrative Assistance 🏢**: Guidance on admissions, registration, fees, and other administrative processes.
- **Campus Events 📅**: Updates on upcoming events, seminars, and workshops within the School of Computing.
- **Concept Explanation 🔍**: Explanations of key computing concepts, theories, and principles.
- **Resource Recommendations 📖**: Suggestions for textbooks, articles, and online resources for further reading and study.
- **Project Assistance 💡**: Tips and guidance for computing projects and experiments.

## Usage 🚀

- **Ask Questions ❓**: Type your question in the chat box and hit enter. SOC Buddy will provide a detailed answer.
- **Get Explanations 🧠**: Request explanations for specific computing concepts or problems.
- **Course Details 🎓**: Inquire about specific courses to get comprehensive information.
- **Event Updates 📅**: Ask about upcoming events and get the latest updates.
- **Learn 📘**: Participate in interactive learning sessions and quizzes to improve your understanding of computing.
