# SOC Buddy — School of Computing Assistant 🎓💻

**SOC Buddy** answers questions about the School of Computing (SOC) at the Federal University of Technology, Akure (FUTA), Nigeria, using the departmental handbooks of Computer Science, Cyber Security, Information Systems, Information Technology and Software Engineering.

## What you can ask 🌟

- **Courses 📚**: course codes, titles, units, and which departments offer a course.
- **Course lists by level and semester**: e.g. "What are the 200 level first semester courses in Software Engineering?"
- **Programme rules**: grading, degree classification, and other rules the handbooks describe.
- **The School**: its departments and history.

Follow-up questions work too: after asking about a course, you can ask "which semester is it taken?".

## Good to know ℹ️

- SOC Buddy only knows what is in the handbooks. If the answer is not there, it will say so. It cannot tell you about events, results, timetables or fees.
- Answers are generated by AI and can be wrong or out of date. Always confirm important details with your department.
- Each account can ask up to 10 questions a minute and 150 a day, so the free service stays available to everyone.
- Forgot your password? Use **Forgot your password?** on the sign-in page to get a reset link by email.
